Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026 · Skyrain

Nutesia is an AI-assisted meal and nutrition tracker made by Skyrain ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address and password if you sign up with email (the password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication and never seen by us), or an anonymous account ID if you continue as a guest.
  • Profile: your name, age, gender, height, weight, goal (lose, maintain or gain weight), and the BMI and daily nutrition targets calculated from them.
  • Food log: the meals you describe or add, their estimated nutrition (calories, macronutrients, vitamins, minerals) and when you logged them.
  • Credits: your credit balance, how credits were earned and spent, and how many rewarded ads you watched each day.
  • Usage and device data: app events such as screens viewed, food items added (name, calories, meal type) and your goal, collected through Google Analytics for Firebase together with device information and an app-instance identifier.
  • Advertising data: when you choose to watch a rewarded ad, Google AdMob collects your device's advertising ID, IP address and device information to show and measure the ad.

How we use it

  • To run the app: keep your profile and food log, calculate your targets and track your credits.
  • To estimate nutrition: the text you type to describe a meal is sent to our AI provider, which returns the estimated foods and nutrients. For the deficiency analysis we send your age, gender, goal, most-eaten foods and average nutrient intake. Your name and email are not sent.
  • To understand and improve the app, using aggregated analytics.
  • To reward you with credits for watching ads.

We do not sell your personal data.

Who processes your data

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Analytics) stores your account, profile, food log and credits. Firebase privacy
  • Groq, Inc. processes meal descriptions and the deficiency-analysis data above to produce nutrition estimates. Groq privacy
  • Google AdMob serves rewarded ads. How Google uses data in ads

These providers may process data outside your country. They act under their own privacy terms and safeguards.

Health information

Your weight, height, BMI and food log can reveal information about your health. We use it only to provide the app's features, never for advertising. Nutrition values are AI estimates and are not medical advice.

How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. When you delete your account, your profile, food log and credit records are deleted immediately, and any copies in our providers' backups expire within their normal backup cycles. Analytics data is kept for the retention period set in Google Analytics (at most 14 months) and is not linked to your name or email.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Profile → Delete account. This deletes your account and all data stored for it. If you can't access the app, see how to request deletion.

Your choices and rights

  • You can edit your profile at any time in the app.
  • You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your device settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data, or to object to its processing. Contact us to exercise these rights.

Children

Nutesia is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored by Google Firebase, which encrypts data at rest.

Changes

We will post updates on this page and change the effective date above. Significant changes will also be announced in the app.
